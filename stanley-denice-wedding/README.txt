STANLEY & DENICE JOY — DIGITAL WEDDING INVITATION

1. Open index.html in a browser to preview the invitation.
2. Upload index.html to your web host or GitHub Pages to publish it.
3. The invitation uses the date Friday, December 18, 2026. Please verify the year before publishing.
4. Venue map buttons open Google Maps searches for the church and reception venue.
5. The RSVP form copies the guest's response; it does not send responses to the couple. Connect a form service or backend to collect RSVPs online.
6. Attire inspiration photos load from Unsplash and require an internet connection. Replace the image URLs with your own approved attire-guide images if preferred.
